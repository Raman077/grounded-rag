# Nimbus Pricing

Nimbus is a managed object storage and compute service. This page lists the plans and how
usage is billed. All prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.

## Plans

| Plan       | Monthly price | Included storage | Included egress | Support          |
|------------|---------------|------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Starter    | $0            | 5 GB             | 10 GB           | Community forum  |
| Pro        | $49           | 500 GB           | 1 TB            | Email, 1 business day |
| Business   | $299          | 5 TB             | 10 TB           | 24/7 chat and phone |
| Enterprise | Custom        | Custom           | Custom          | Dedicated technical account manager |

Annual billing gives a 15% discount on the Pro and Business plans.

## Overage charges

Storage above the included amount costs $0.018 per GB-month. Egress above the included
amount costs $0.05 per GB. Overage is billed at the end of each calendar month.

## Free trial

New accounts get a 30-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required to
start the trial. At the end of the trial the account moves to the Starter plan unless a paid
plan is chosen.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 60 days. Monthly plans are not
refundable, but they can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the
billing period.
