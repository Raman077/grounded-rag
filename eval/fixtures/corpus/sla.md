# Service Level Agreement

This agreement describes the availability Nimbus commits to and the credits customers
receive when it is missed.

## Availability commitment

Nimbus commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for the Pro plan and 99.95% for the Business plan.
Enterprise contracts include 99.99% monthly uptime. The Starter plan has no SLA.

Uptime is measured per region from Nimbus's external probes every 60 seconds.

## Service credits

| Monthly uptime          | Credit (% of monthly fee) |
|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| Below commitment, ≥ 99.0% | 10%                     |
| 95.0% – 99.0%           | 25%                       |
| Below 95.0%             | 50%                       |

Credits must be requested through the support portal within 30 days of the end of the
affected month. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out as cash.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime.
Maintenance windows are limited to 4 hours per month and run between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC.
